Device sharing

The SDK supports two ways to share a device: by QR code, and by account. However, if the app does not use Gwell’s account service and sets disableAccountService = true at initialization, the SDK hides account sharing by default. To show it, the app follows the steps below.

Implement and register the interfaces

Section titled “Implement and register the interfaces”

The SDK queries the app to confirm and display the account information of shared users, such as nickname and avatar. Two scenarios query with different parameters:

  1. In the share flow, query account information by the account the user enters.
  2. To show the list of users a device is shared with, query account information by a list of Gwell accessIds.

Your app and your cloud must implement these two interfaces, and the account information includes the Gwell accessId. So when the customer cloud completes account authentication integration with Gwell Cloud, it must map and store the Gwell accessId against the app account.

Note: the SDK uses the nickname and avatar only for display in the UI. It does not cache them or upload them to any server. The app can mask the nickname before passing it to the SDK, for example test***1.

Acceptance

Section titled “Acceptance”
  • The share entry shows both account sharing and QR code sharing.
  • Entering a mapped account shows its nickname and avatar.
  • After sharing, the share list shows the nickname and avatar of each shared user.
  • When deviceShareOptions lists only QR code sharing, the account sharing entry disappears.
